This policy describes what IELTS Prep ("the app", "we", "us") actually collects, stores, and does with your data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the IELTS Prep mobile app, operated by [Company/Owner Legal Name], [Business Address]. Contact: support@ieltsprep.app.

2. Two modes the app can run in

  • Demo Mode (no backend configured): every piece of data below stays entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to us or any third party except the AI providers used for evaluation/transcription (see §4), and only when you use a feature that calls one.
  • Signed-in Mode (a real account): the same data categories are stored in our database, in rows scoped to your account and isolated from other users by database-level access rules (Row Level Security).

4. Third parties that actually process your data

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and serverless functions backing Signed-in Mode.
  • AI providers (whichever one is configured server-side — never more than one active at a time): OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google (Gemini). Submitting a Writing essay, a Speaking recording, an AI Coach message, or a study-plan request sends the relevant text to whichever provider is active, solely to generate that response. See OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google's own privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — if configured, manages subscription/purchase state; see RevenueCat's policy.
  • Google Play Billing — the actual payment processor; we never see or store your payment card details.

No crash-reporting, analytics, or advertising SDK is present in this app.

5. Microphone and audio recordings (Speaking practice)

Audio is recorded only while you are actively recording a response you started — never in the background, never before that explicit action. The recording is held temporarily on your device, then uploaded to our server-side function for transcription by the configured AI provider; the resulting text (not the raw audio) is what gets evaluated.

We do not upload your audio to any persistent server-side storage bucket of our own. We cannot guarantee the AI provider's own retention of a request it processed — see §4. We do not claim the local recording file is deleted from your device immediately after upload.

6. AI-generated feedback — limitations

Every score and piece of AI-generated feedback (Writing, Speaking, AI Coach, study-plan suggestions) is an estimate for practice purposes, not an official IELTS score, and not affiliated with any organization that administers the real exam. AI evaluation can be wrong or inconsistent.

7. Local device storage

Independent of any account: in-progress onboarding answers, draft essay text, and, in Demo Mode, your entire local dataset are stored only on your device.

8. Notifications

If enabled, the app schedules local, on-device reminders (daily study, streak, weekly summary, exam countdown) via your device's own notification system — not push notifications through a remote server, and no notification token is collected. Disable any category anytime; the app works fully without notification permission.

9. Error logging

Diagnostic log lines are written to standard platform/Supabase function logs for our own debugging — not a dedicated third-party crash-reporting service.

10. Data retention and deletion

Delete your account and all associated data anytime from Profile → Delete account in the app. Deletion removes your authentication record and every database row tied to your account, is immediate and irreversible, and may not remove data already sent to a third-party AI provider before deletion. We may retain minimal records where legally required (e.g. tax records for a completed payment). If you can't sign in, see our account deletion request page.

11. Children

Not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect data from them.

12. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data. Deletion is available in-app; for anything else, contact us.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent revision.

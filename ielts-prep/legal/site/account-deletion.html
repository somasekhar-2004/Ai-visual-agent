Delete your IELTS Prep account

Last updated: 2026-09-18

This page exists for the case where you cannot open the app or sign in — for example, you uninstalled it, forgot your password, or lost access to your device. If you can still sign in, deleting from inside the app (Profile → Delete account, or Help & support → Delete account) is faster and takes effect immediately.

How to request deletion from inside the app (preferred)

  1. Open IELTS Prep and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (or Profile → Help & support).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the warning, then confirm by tapping Delete account again in the confirmation dialog.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

How to request deletion if you can't sign in

Email support@ieltsprep.app from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line Account deletion request. Include:

  • The email address your account was registered with.
  • A statement that you want your account and its data permanently deleted.

We will verify the request is genuinely from the account owner before deleting it, and will confirm once the deletion is complete. [State your target response time here, e.g. "within 30 days," once you've decided what you can actually commit to.]

What gets deleted

Requesting deletion (either path above) permanently removes:

  • Your authentication record (email, login credentials).
  • Your profile, study goal, and target/current band settings.
  • Every practice attempt, Full Mock attempt, and question-level attempt across Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking.
  • Your Writing submissions and AI feedback, Speaking submissions and AI feedback.
  • Band score history, streak, XP, and achievements.
  • Bookmarks, vocabulary/grammar progress, and study-plan data.
  • AI Coach conversation history.
  • Your subscription record and daily AI-usage log entries.

What may be retained

  • Records we are legally required to keep — for example, financial/tax records tied to a completed subscription payment — for the period the law requires, even after your account is deleted.
  • Anything already sent to and retained by a third-party AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google, depending on which was configured) as part of processing a request before your account was deleted — governed by that provider's own retention policy, not ours. See our Privacy Policy for which providers are actually used.
